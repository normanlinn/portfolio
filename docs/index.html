02 / Hi, I’m Zaw Lin.

A different kind of background.
The same attention to detail.

I'm Zaw Lin Naing, also known as Norman Linn. I come from Myanmar and live in Los Angeles, where I work in aviation customer service and study at El Camino College.

Airport work is full of changing plans, clear communication, and helping people find their next step. That experience shapes how I build: notice the friction, keep the important things clear, and keep improving.

Alongside web development, I'm learning C++ and data structures. These projects are my way of turning that curiosity into things people can use.
